# CRO audit: katarinasvitek.com

*Run with the cro-website skill, 20 Nov 2026. Reviewed from page text (copy, structure, conversion paths), not visual design or mobile layout.*

**Assumed setup:** one-page freelance homepage. Conversion = intro-call request via the contact form. Traffic mostly LinkedIn and direct.

## Biggest issue: who the site is for
The whole site sells a freelancer, while I'm also applying for full-time roles. A hiring manager clicking through from my CV sees "Freelance growth and GTM marketer" and "available for freelance projects", which can read as "not looking for a job". If the site serves both audiences, one line fixes it: *"Open to freelance projects and senior in-house roles."*

## Quick wins
1. **"Book an intro call" doesn't book anything.** Both CTAs jump to a form. Embed a calendar (Cal.com or Calendly) or rename the button to "Get in touch".
2. **Say what the results were for.** These are in-house results. Add "as Senior Digital Marketing Manager" (or similar) to each result so a freelance buyer doesn't wonder about client work.
3. **Add "previously at" names** (LiveWell by Zurich, Gowago, Cytosurge) near the hero. There are no logos or testimonials on the page. Check brand-use rules before using actual logos.
4. **Form fixes:**
   - Change "I'm interested in" to the engagement options (Growth audit / Project sprint / Fractional lead / Not sure yet) instead of Personal vs Business GTM.
   - Make "Company or role" optional.
   - Change the button from "Send message →" to "Request my intro call".
   - Add *"I reply within one business day. No newsletter, no spam."* under the button.
5. **Get one or two testimonials** from a former manager or agency partner and place them next to the form.

## High-impact changes
1. **The H1 is jargon.** "GTM Engineering in the age of AI" doesn't tell a founder what they get. Lead with the outcome and put the proof right under it.
2. **Make the Growth audit the entry offer.** Give it its own block before Contact with fixed scope, a timeline (e.g. "delivered in 5 days") and a starting price or "from CHF X". Price is the biggest unanswered question, and there's no FAQ.
3. **Narrow or reorder the two services.** Every result supports Business GTM; Personal GTM has no proof yet. Lead with Business GTM.
4. **Reorder sections:** Hero → Results → How we can work together (audit, sprint, fractional) → Why me → Services → Contact.

## Test ideas
- Outcome headline vs. the current "GTM Engineering" H1
- Inline calendar booking vs. the form
- Growth audit as primary CTA vs. intro call
- Showing a starting price vs. leaving it out

## Copy alternatives
**Headline**
- *"I take products to market, then build the AI agents that keep them growing."* Clear about what I do, keeps the AI edge.
- *"15K → 500K users. −91% CPA. Now working with growth teams like yours."* Proof first, for skeptical buyers.
- *"Senior performance marketing, with AI-native execution."* Short; works for freelance buyers and hiring managers alike.

**Primary CTA**
- "Book a 20-min intro call" (only with a real calendar)
- "Get a growth audit"
- "Tell me your key number" (echoes the contact intro)
